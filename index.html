Common questions

Do I have to replace my septic tank before I sell?

If your septic tank discharges directly to a ditch, stream or other watercourse, it doesn't meet the General Binding Rules and must be upgraded or replaced. Buyer and seller should agree who does this as part of the sale, and lenders may refuse a mortgage until it's resolved. We can tell you where you stand and what the fix would cost.

What is a percolation test?

It measures how quickly your soil absorbs water. The result tells us whether a drainage field will work on your site and how large it needs to be. Building control, and increasingly planning officers, ask for it before approving a new system.

What if my ground doesn't pass?

Heavy clay or a high water table doesn't mean you're stuck. Options include a raised mound, a different location on the site, or a treatment plant discharging elsewhere with the right permit. We'll set out what's possible.

How long does an installation take?

Most domestic installations take a few days on site once approvals are in place. Very wet weather can delay work on clay ground, and we'll plan around it.
